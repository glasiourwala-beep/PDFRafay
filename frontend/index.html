The All-in-One Online PDF Suite for Everyday Workflow

PDFRafay is a complete online PDF processing web app engineered for students, professionals, teams, and business workflows. Whether you need to convert Microsoft Word documents to PDF for official submissions, reduce PDF file size for email attachments, or merge multiple contracts into one document, PDFRafay delivers fast, server-side conversions without compromising quality.

Convert Word, PowerPoint & Images to PDF

Transform Office documents (.doc, .docx, .ppt, .pptx) and digital photos (.jpg, .png, .webp) into pristine, universally viewable PDF files. Server-side rendering guarantees font accuracy, image fidelity, and exact alignment across Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, and Linux devices.

Compress PDF Files Without Quality Loss

Large PDF files can be difficult to email or upload to online portals. Our PDF compression engine optimizes document streams and image assets, reducing file sizes by up to 80% while retaining sharp text readability.

Organize, Split, Merge & Rearrange Pages

Take total control of your document structure. Combine separate PDFs into a unified file, extract specific page ranges (e.g., 1-5, 8-10), delete blank pages, or flip upside-down scans by rotating pages 90° or 180°.

Protect and Secure PDF Documents

Keep confidential files safe. Stamp custom text watermarks (e.g. "CONFIDENTIAL" or "DRAFT") across pages, encrypt documents with robust passwords, or unlock password-protected files whenever authorized.
